Sequence Puzzle Game

Test your pattern recognition and logical reasoning with Sequence, a free online sequence puzzle game from ThunderQuiz. Study the numbers, symbols, or shapes, discover the hidden rule, and choose what comes next.

Each round challenges you to spot relationships and patterns under time pressure. Start with simpler sequences and work your way toward increasingly difficult challenges as you improve your score and build your streak.

How to Play Sequence

  1. Study the sequence shown on the screen.
  2. Look for the rule connecting the pieces.
  3. Work out what should come next.
  4. Choose the missing piece from the available answers.
  5. Keep playing to improve your score and streak.

What Are Sequence Puzzles?

Sequence puzzles are logic and pattern-recognition challenges where you identify the rule connecting a series of numbers, symbols, shapes, or other elements. The goal is to use the information provided to predict the missing or next element.

Some sequence puzzles use simple arithmetic such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division. Others use repeating patterns, alternating rules, changes in position, or multiple relationships working together.

What Skills Does Sequence Test?

Sequence focuses on pattern recognition, observation, logical reasoning, problem solving, and quick decision-making. As the difficulty increases, you may need to compare several parts of a sequence before identifying the rule.

Tips for Solving Sequence Puzzles

  • Check whether the difference between numbers stays constant.
  • Look for multiplication or division patterns.
  • Check whether two or more patterns alternate.
  • Look for repeating groups or cycles.
  • Compare adjacent elements as well as the sequence as a whole.
  • If the obvious rule does not work, try looking for a second-order pattern.
